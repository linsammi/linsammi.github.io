I chose engineering because I have always liked understanding how things work. I decided to study computer engineering because computers and artificial intelligence are becoming such a large part of our future, and I felt that the field would provide many career opportunities and job security. As I have gone further into engineering, I have become especially interested in artificial intelligence, robotics, and hardware. I enjoy being able to understand software and also how it interacts with something physical.

One experience that has strengthened my interest in technology has been working with drones and being part of a larger engineering team. It is exciting knowing that every person can contribute a smaller part that eventually becomes something much bigger. I have also worked on an AI project focused on helping students use AI as a learning tool rather than allowing it to simply do their work for them. Since students are likely to continue using AI, I think it is important to find ways for it to support learning instead of becoming a student's downfall.

In the future, I want to develop skills that allow me to work on real world technology. I am particularly interested in projects involving AI, robotics, software, and hardware because I like seeing technology have a physical or practical purpose. I also want to gain more experience working on large teams and improve my technical skills through real projects rather than only classroom assignments. Having experience in different areas of computer and electrical engineering would also give me the flexibility to discover which area I ultimately enjoy the most.

In my future career, I could see myself working in hardware or software engineering. Ultimately, I hope to develop both the technical and leadership skills that will allow me to contribute to meaningful engineering projects and eventually take on greater responsibility within a team.
