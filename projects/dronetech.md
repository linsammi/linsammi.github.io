UH Drone Technologies gave me the opportunity to gain hands-on engineering experience outside of the classroom. As part of the Power and Propulsion team, I contributed to the development of a prototype autonomous drone designed for UXO detection and retrieval. My work included mass budgeting, battery and power-system design, and hardware integration.

I also gained exposure to the integration of technologies such as LiDAR, radar, infrared, and EMI sensors. Working with a multidisciplinary team helped me understand how electrical, mechanical, and software components must work together to create a functioning autonomous system.

In addition to my technical work, I served in an administrative role where I helped coordinate recruitment, events, and team documentation. This experience allowed me to develop both my engineering and leadership skills while working on a real-world drone system.
